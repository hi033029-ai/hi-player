# Hi Player demo privacy notice

This notice describes the behavior implemented in the Hi Player demo source. It is **not a claim of Google verification, a security certification, or completed production legal review**. Do not substitute this notice for review of a future production app's actual behavior.

## Data used by features
- **Local media and files:** with Android permissions or a user-selected document/folder grant, the app scans media metadata, displays a library, plays selected files, and manages user-requested downloads/files. Library metadata, playback history, browser history/bookmarks/cookies, and settings may be stored on the device.
- **Google sign-in:** Google and Firebase Authentication process sign-in credentials and account identifiers. Hi Player displays the returned profile name, email address and photo and uses the Firebase account identifier/session. The app does not ask the user to type a Google password into Hi Player and does not copy Google website cookies into its embedded browser. Firebase/Google process authentication data under their own policies; signing out locally is not the same as deleting a Firebase account record.
- **Optional Google Drive demo:** only after the user chooses My Drive and grants Google's separate permission, the app requests `drive.readonly`. This scope can read/list/download the account's Drive files; the app's browser filters its display to folders and supported video/audio media. It checks that the Drive API account matches the signed-in Google account and streams a file selected by the user. This implementation does not request file write/delete permissions or upload local files to Drive.
- **Drive credentials and media:** the demo holds short-lived Drive access tokens in memory, not in playback URLs/history, browser cookies, or a general remote-header registry. Drive credentials are sent to Google's HTTPS Drive API; redirected CDN requests do not receive the bearer header. Playback buffers media, and playback history can retain a file's name/identifier/clean playback URL. A local disconnect clears the app's current Drive session/list; it does not revoke Google's existing authorization.
- **Other optional online services:** websites and download servers receive requested network traffic. Lyric features query LRCLIB and lyrics.ovh using track metadata; browser filter updates contact their configured list providers. User-configured Jellyfin/WebDAV servers receive requests and their own credentials. Those operators have their own privacy policies and may receive request metadata such as IP address. No claim is made that an ad blocker prevents every third-party request.

## Storage, retention and controls
Local media caches, history, settings, cookies, downloads and server configuration remain until cleared, removed, overwritten, uninstalled, or otherwise managed by the relevant Android/provider lifecycle. Android backup/device-transfer behavior depends on system settings and app backup rules; do not assume all app state is excluded from backup. User-configured Jellyfin/WebDAV credentials use the app's encrypted preference store; the Android keystore and device security still matter.

You can decline optional permissions, sign out of Hi Player, disconnect the local Drive session, clear browser data, manage downloads/files, and revoke app access in your Google Account settings. Disconnecting/signing out does not erase previously downloaded local files or delete the underlying Google Drive files. For app/Firebase account deletion requests or questions, contact the configured project support address: **hi033029@gmail.com**. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or private media in a support request.

## Limits of this demo
The demo's Drive permission is unverified and may be warned about or blocked by Google. These demo pages do not replace Google's restricted-scope/branding review, any required security assessment, or the owner's production privacy obligations. No claim is made that this document covers changes outside the inspected demo source or all behavior of third-party websites/services. A future production release needs an accurate reviewed notice and any required verified domains/consent demo.
